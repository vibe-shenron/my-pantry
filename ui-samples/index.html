My Pantry: three UI directions

They all use the same sample data, and everything works: +/− buttons, search, filters, tapping an item, the + add button, ticking the shopping list and restocking, Sync now, and pairing. Nothing is saved, so reload to reset. For the real feel, open one full screen on your phone.

APantry Shelf

Warm and homey. Items are cards on a shelf, with a Home screen that tells you what needs doing today.

  • Best for browsing and seeing things at a glance
  • Fewer items per screen (2-column cards)

BClean List

Looks like a built-in phone app. It opens straight on the list, grouped by location. Dark mode follows your phone's setting.

  • Fastest: most items per screen, fewest taps
  • Plainer: no dashboard

CDashboard

Dark and data-forward. Big numbers, a stock meter on every item, location tiles, and the lowest stock sorted to the top.

  • Best for seeing how full everything is
  • Always dark; busier to look at
